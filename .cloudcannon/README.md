# Welcome to Sendit

This is the Sendit marketing website: a home page, feature, pricing, about and contact pages, a privacy page, and a blog.

## Pages

Home, Feature, Pricing, About and Contact are built from **sections** (a hero, a feature row, a pricing table, an FAQ and so on).

- Open a page to edit it on the live preview. Click any heading, paragraph, button or image to change it.
- Use the **+** buttons between sections to add a new section, and drag sections to reorder them.
- Lists inside a section (team members, testimonials, FAQ questions, pricing tiers, counter figures) can be added to, reordered and removed in the same way.
- To make a new page, click **+ Add** in the Pages list and choose **Page builder page**. Until the new page is saved and built, the preview shows the Feature page's layout.

The **Privacy** page is a legal page made of titled sections, each a set of paragraphs or bullet points. Create more pages like it with **+ Add → Legal page**.

## Blog

- Open a post to edit its title, author, featured image and text on the page, or switch to the Content Editor to write.
- New posts open in the Content Editor and start as drafts. Draft posts aren't published, so they can't be previewed on the page until you turn **Draft** off.
- Pick categories from the list. The list itself lives in **Site data → Blog categories**.
- The Blog listing page (the heading and intro at the top of /blog/) is the **Blog listing page** file in the Blog collection.

## Site data

These settings appear on every page:

- **Navigation** — the logo, menu items, dropdowns and the optional button in the header.
- **Footer** — the footer logo, copyright line, social links and the columns of links.
- **Site SEO** — default description, share image and Twitter username used when a page doesn't set its own.
- **Blog categories** — the categories available on blog posts.

The header and footer can also be edited by clicking them on any page's preview.
